Flixly

Apni clips (jitni chahiye, koi fix limit nahi) caption aur music ke sath jod kar aik polished, Reels-ready vertical video banao — seedha browser mein, koi heavy processing nahi.

Ek honest baat: koi bhi tool Instagram ke algorithm par "boost/viral" ki guarantee nahi de sakta. Lekin jo cheezein editing se control ho sakti hain — sahi 9:16 size, smooth transitions, hook par punch-in, tight trimming, background music — woh sab is tool mein hain. Yeh tool ab seedha aapke browser ke apne recorder se kaam karta hai (koi bhari wasm engine nahi), is liye generate hamesha video ki length ke barabar time mein khatam ho jata hai aur crash nahi hota.

Target total: sec
Yeh sirf final judi hui video ki total length control karta hai — chahe kitni bhi clips ho, aakhri video kabhi is number se lambi nahi banegi. "Auto-fit" sab clips ko is total mein barabar hisso mein baant deta hai (sirf ek shuruaati andaza, aap trim start/end se har clip ko khud bhi adjust kar sakte ho). Note: yeh har clip ke andar wala "Best len" box (Auto-select best part ke liye) se bilkul alag cheez hai — woh sirf usi ek clip ke andar sab se acha/loud hissa dhoondta hai, poori video ki length usse control nahi hoti.
Quality & output
HD Smart 1080×1920 (Reels/Insta ka standard best) target karta hai, aur khud aapke device ki speed bhanp kar bitrate/fps thoda adjust kar leta hai — taake low-RAM phone ho ya tez laptop, dono par smoothly bane, bina kisi extra setting ke.
